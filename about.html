Who We Are

THE NEXUS
ORIGIN STORY

Founded in 2016 by former professional athlete Marcus Dean, NEXUS GYM was born from a frustration with mediocre fitness experiences. Marcus envisioned a space where elite training methodology met world-class facilities — accessible to everyone willing to put in the work.

What started as a 2,000 sq ft studio has evolved into a 50,000 sq ft performance campus with over 3,500 members and 12 of the most credentialed coaches in the industry. Our obsession hasn't changed: extraordinary results for extraordinary people.
