FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Is there a joining fee?+

No joining fees ever. Your first week is completely free with no commitment required. We let our results speak for themselves.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Absolutely. All memberships are month-to-month. Cancel anytime without penalties. We believe in earning your loyalty every single month.

What are your opening hours?+

We're open 24/7 for Pro and Elite members. Starter members have access from 6 AM to 10 PM daily including weekends.

Do you offer nutrition coaching?+

Yes! Our Pro and Elite memberships include personalized nutrition guidance. We also offer standalone nutrition coaching packages for non-members.

Is the gym suitable for beginners?+

100%. We welcome all fitness levels. Every new member receives a free orientation session to learn the equipment and create a personalized training plan.
